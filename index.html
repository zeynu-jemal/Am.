About AL-NOOR Portal

Portal Overview

AL-NOOR Islamic Portal is your complete digital companion designed to enrich your daily spiritual routine. It seamlessly integrates the Holy Quran with multiple reciters, authenticated Hadith collections, precise city-based prayer schedules, Qibla tracking, and morning/evening duas.

Customization Features

  • Theme Customizer: Personalize navigation bars and theme accent colors to suit your taste.
  • Dynamic Backgrounds: Load custom background images or looping video links using the BG Customizer panel.
  • Fluid Navigation: Easy touch-responsive fluid motion menu layout for quick access across all utilities.
